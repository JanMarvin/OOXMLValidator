# Excel deviations from ECMA-376 (MS-OI29500)

[MS-OI29500] lists where Office implements ISO/IEC 29500 differently. The
validator does not know these notes: a file can be schema and semantically
valid and still be rejected or handled differently by Excel. This file
collects the notes that apply to what openxlsx2, encharter and easeling
write, and what was done about them. Reviewed against the 2026-08-18
revision.

## How the notes were selected

All notes of Part 1 sections 2, 9, 12, 14.2, 15, 18, 20, 21 and 22, Part 2,
Part 3 and Part 4 sections 8, 19 and 20 were extracted (2760 notes). They
were matched against the elements and attributes found in fuzzing corpora of
the three packages (2500 openxlsx2, 1500 encharter and 600 easeling cases),
which leaves 720 notes on content the packages write. The notes that state a
limit or a requirement were checked against the corpora and, for chart data
labels, against a grid of 14 chart types x 2 groupings x 9 label positions.

## Fixed

| Note | Package | Rule | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Part 1 21.2.2.48 | encharter | `dLblPos` inside a series only for the positions a chart group accepts; none for area, radar, doughnut and 3D groups | positions outside the allowed set are left out, Excel uses its default |
| Part 1 21.2.2.187 | encharter | `showPercent` only below pie, pie3D, ofPie and doughnut charts | written as 1 only for these |
| Part 1 21.2.2.178 | encharter | `showBubbleSize` only below bubble charts | written as 1 only for these |
| Part 1 21.2.2.156 | encharter | rich text of a title has at most 255 characters | titles are checked |
| Part 1 21.2.2.196, 21.2.3.45 | encharter | Office does not allow `auto`, without `splitType` Excel splits by position at a third of the points | `auto` omits `splitType` and `splitPos` |
| Part 1 21.2.2.195 | encharter | `splitPos` needs `splitType` `percent`, `pos` or `val`; a position split is a whole number up to 32000, a percent split up to 100 | checked |
| Part 1 18.3.1.38 ff. | openxlsx2 | headers and footers have at most 255 characters | checked, section and field codes included |
| Part 1 18.3.1.32 | openxlsx2 | data validation: prompt 255, error 224, titles 32 characters | checked |
| Part 1 18.2.30 | openxlsx2 | `windowHeight` and `windowWidth` at most 2147483647 | range of `set_bookview()` |
| Part 1 18.2.27 | openxlsx2 | Excel cannot print a workbook without `bookViews` | `remove_bookview()` keeps a default view, removing a sheet before the first save no longer drops it |
| Part 1 18.8.31 | openxlsx2 | number format codes have less than 255 characters | checked |
| Part 1 18.3.1.73, 18.3.1.14 | openxlsx2 | less than 1023 row breaks, at most 1023 column breaks | `add_page_break()` stops there |
| Part 1 18.4.7 | openxlsx2 | `rPr` children in the order b, i, strike, condense, extend, outline, shadow, u, vertAlign, sz, color, rFont, family, charset, scheme | `fmt_txt()` and comments write this order |

## Open decisions

| Note | Package | Rule | Current state |
|---|---|---|---|
| Part 1 18.8.18 | openxlsx2 | Excel restricts font `family` to 0 to 5 | 10 to 14 are written and ignored in `excel-compat.ignore` |
| Part 1 18.3.1.96, 18.4.12 | openxlsx2 | cell values and strings have at most 32767 characters | not checked by openxlsx2, the fuzzer writes longer inline strings |
| Part 1 18.8.31 | openxlsx2 | at most 206 custom number formats load | not checked by openxlsx2 |
| Part 1 18.8.23, 18.3.1.73 | openxlsx2 | at most 65491 fonts, cell style index at most 65490 | not checked by openxlsx2 |
| Part 1 18.3.1.10 | openxlsx2 | `operator` only on `cellIs` rules | text rules write `operator` like Excel itself does |
| Part 1 2.2 | all | Office rejects `INF` and `NaN` in double values | not seen in the corpora, the packages check for finite numbers |
| Part 2 | all | Office does not load part names with non ASCII characters | media parts are numbered, not seen |
| Part 3 | all | no markup compatibility in the docProps parts | not seen |

## Checked and clean

DrawingML (Part 1 20.1, 20.5): `ds` dash and space lengths, anchor `to`
before `from`, anchor rows and columns, `macro` length, `textlink` without
`txBody` (only in a file written by Excel, with an empty link), `schemeClr`
inside `clrScheme`.

SpreadsheetML (Part 1 18): `font` child order in styles, font names, sheet
names unique ignoring case, `sheetId` and `localSheetId` ranges, column
widths, `baseColWidth`, `pageSetup` ranges, panes, `cfRule` type, formula
and `timePeriod`, table and table column names, `totalsRowCount`, cell style
names and `builtinId`, `colorId`, `reservationPassword` with `userName`.

Charts (Part 1 21.2): axis ids and cross axes, at most 4 axes, 255 series
and 254 trendlines, unique series `idx` and `order`, `val` and `yVal`,
formula length, `numCache` values, `holeSize`, `serLines`, `bubble3D`,
markers on 3D lines, `grpFill`, chart group `dLbls` children, data points.

A fuzzing run with 600 cases per package on the patched packages has no
finding left apart from the open decisions above.
